CDL CLASS A LICENSE

  • What type of CDL do I need?

    The type of license you need will vary based on the kind of driving career you want to pursue. At Roadmaster, our courses focus on the Class A CDL, which is essential for over-the-road (OTR) professional truck drivers. – Click here to learn more about CDL licenses.

  • Class A Commercial Driver’s License

    A Class A Commercial Driver’s License (CDL) demonstrates to carriers and employers that you are a qualified and skilled professional truck driver. Driving specific commercial vehicles requires advanced expertise and training. Click here to learn more about Class A CDL licenses.

  • Why do I need a Class A CDL?

    The Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Act ensures highway safety by requiring drivers of large trucks and buses to be properly qualified. The Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) mandates that Class A CDLs are issued only after drivers pass specific knowledge and skills tests. A CDL is essential for operating commercial vehicles across interstate, intrastate, or foreign commerce.

  • How do I get my Class A CDL?

    To obtain a Class A CDL, you must graduate from an ELDT-compliant truck driving school and pass both a written and driving test administered by the state or an approved facility. It's important to choose a certified school that meets the ELDT requirements. The FMCSA provides a list of qualified training providers through the Training Provider Registry (TPR).
