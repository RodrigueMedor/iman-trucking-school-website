Amazon Career Choice CDL Program

Career Choice is a benefit offered by Amazon to eligible associates, aimed at expanding their career opportunities by equipping them with the necessary resources and skills for high-demand, well-paying jobs—whether within Amazon or in other industries.

The transportation sector, particularly truck driving, presents excellent career prospects for those with a Commercial Driver’s License (CDL). Take advantage of the Amazon Career Choice Program and discover how it can cover your tuition at Iman Trucking School, helping you start a rewarding career in the trucking industry.

How the Amazon Career Choice Program Works

If you’re looking to start a career in truck driving or another high-demand field, the Amazon Career Choice Programcan provide full or partial tuition assistance to help you achieve your goals. As long as your chosen program falls within Amazon’s list of in-demand careers—such as commercial truck driving—you may qualify for financial support.

Most programs covered by Amazon Career Choice include vocational certifications and two-year associate degrees. In addition to transportation, the program also supports training in industries like computer science, electrical and mechanical trades, construction, information technology, and healthcare. If you’re interested in earning your Commercial Driver’s License (CDL), you can use this opportunity to enroll in Iman Trucking School and begin your journey toward a rewarding trucking career.

Amazon Career Choice Benefits

The benefits of the Amazon Career Choice Program vary depending on program eligibility. If you’re enrolling in a program like Iman Trucking School to receive your CDL, you could qualify for 100% tuition coverage. For other programs, you may receive partial assistance for tuition, books, and fees.

How to Become Eligible for the Program

To apply for tuition coverage through Amazon Career Choice, you must first be employed as an hourly Amazon employee for at least 90 days. The specific programs covered by this benefit may vary, so if you’re applying for a program not currently in the approved partner network, you might need to submit an application or wait for approval.

While salaried Amazon employees are not currently eligible for this program, employees who are promoted during their studies will not lose their tuition assistance. Instead, they can continue to receive funding for their program until its completion.

CDL Training at Iman Trucking School

Iman Trucking School’s Class A CDL Training Program equips students with the essential skills needed to obtain a Class A CDL and qualify for high-paying truck driving positions.

As an Iman Trucking School student, your training will include:

  • Hands-on driving experience at our practice facility and on the road, in real-world conditions
  • Pre-Trip Inspection Training to successfully pass the required test
  • Map reading, trip planning, and ensuring compliance with Department of Transportation laws
  • Backing, turning, and hooking/unhooking an industry-standard 53ft trailer

Truck Driving Career Benefits

Entering the truck driving profession comes with numerous advantages, including:

  • Great Pay – Truck driving jobs offer competitive starting wages.
  • Job Security – Truck driving jobs are essential and can’t be outsourced.
  • Medical & Dental Insurance – Comprehensive medical and dental coverage is a common perk in the industry.
  • 401k Retirement Plan & Life Insurance – Enjoy retirement savings options and competitive life insurance plans.
  • Changing Scenery – Travel across the country while earning a living!
  • Take Your Pet with You! – Some carriers allow drivers to bring their pets along for the journey.
